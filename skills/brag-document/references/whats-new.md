# What's new

What changed for the person in each version, in plain words, newest first. Read it only to tell the person about an update (SKILL.md, "Start of every run"). Pick the one to three changes they will notice and say them in their language; skip internal fixes. Release notes for developers are in the repository's CHANGELOG.md.

The heading of the next version stays `Unreleased` until a release gives it a number.

## 0.2.2

- The interactive page opens in your browser by itself when it is ready. Before, an app's side panel could show it blank.
- After an update, the skill tells you in a line or two what is new, like this.
- A highlight such as "0 de 11" keeps its whole sentence under the number.

## 0.2.1

- Ask "how are my connections?" to see which sources work and what to do about the ones that don't.
- Highlight numbers no longer pick a name like "fase 2" or a horizon like "next 2 quarters" as a result.

## 0.2.0

- Works in Cowork: the config and the log can live in a folder you share.
- Setup asks you to confirm your timezone.
